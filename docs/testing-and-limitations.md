# Testing and limitations

This page distinguishes documented checks from future validation. No product
test logs, accessibility audit, or participant research records were supplied
with this repository.

## Documented founder-led checks

No specific founder-led usability or functional checks can be documented from
the available evidence. The project brief identifies founder-led testing as a
work responsibility, but does not provide test cases, dates, findings, or
records. This is not a claim that no checks took place; their results cannot be
substantiated here.

Founder-led review, when documented, is not equivalent to participatory
research with disabled people.

## Known implementation limitations

- Ultravocal 4.0 is described in the brief as an early-stage product.
- No application source or implementation evidence is present in this
  repository.
- No test records or assistive-technology validation results were supplied.
- The existence of accessibility settings does not establish that they are
  applied correctly or that the experience is accessible.

No additional implementation limitations are asserted without supporting
records.

## Questions for participatory validation

Future research with disabled participants should explore:

- Are preference categories and their descriptions understandable?
- Can people locate and change the settings they need?
- Do presets help people get started, or create unnecessary constraints?
- Do settings remain understandable across different interface contexts?
- Does the experience work with relevant assistive technologies?
- Do the choices support people with multiple or changing access needs?

These are research questions, not completed findings. Functional testing can
verify that controls behave as specified; it cannot substitute for learning
from disabled people about whether the choices, language, and experience work
for them.
